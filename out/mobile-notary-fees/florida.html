Mobile Notary Fees in Florida: What to Charge in 2026

Here's what mobile notaries and loan signing agents typically charge in Florida in 2026 — the state fee cap, realistic local pricing, and how loan signing rates compare.

Quick Answer for Florida

State fee cap: $10 per signature (Online: $25)

Typical local mobile appointment: $50-$95 total (fee + travel)

Typical loan signing rate: $100-$175 per appointment

This page is a Florida-specific companion to the complete notary pricing guide, which covers the full 50-state fee chart, travel fee structure, and pricing calculator.

This is the maximum fee per notarial act. You can charge less, but never more. Data current as of January 2026 — verify with Florida's Secretary of State or notary regulatory authority, since fees can change annually.

Florida's $10 base fee (or $25 for online notarization) covers a real slice of the job, so you're not relying on travel fees alone the way a $2 or $5 state would.

What Mobile Notaries Actually Charge in Florida

The state fee is only one piece of the total price. A realistic local mobile appointment combines the per-signature fee with a travel charge for your time and mileage.

Typical Florida Pricing

  • General notarization + travel: $50-$95
  • Loan signing appointment: $100-$175
  • After-hours/weekend: 1.5x-2x your standard rate

South Florida markets (Miami, Fort Lauderdale) tend to run toward the higher end of the range given cost of living and signing volume; smaller inland markets often land closer to the lower end. Common Florida markets include Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum notary fee in Florida?

Florida caps the notarial fee at $10 for an acknowledgment and $10 for a jurat (Online: $25). This is the per-signature cap, separate from any travel fee.

How much does a mobile notary charge in Florida?

A typical local mobile notary appointment in Florida runs about $50-$95, once the state fee and a reasonable travel charge are combined.

What do loan signing agents charge in Florida?

Loan signing rates in Florida typically run $100-$175 per appointment, depending on distance, timing, and scan-back requirements.

Can notaries charge a travel fee in Florida?

Yes. Travel fees are charged separately from the notarial fee in Florida, the same as in most states, and are a major part of mobile notary pricing.
