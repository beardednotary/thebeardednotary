Mobile Notary Supplies Checklist

This page is meant to help you build a setup that actually supports the work. Instead of treating supplies like a random checklist, think of them in layers: legal essentials, mobile workflow tools, day-to-day business tools, and the upgrades that matter once loan signing work starts growing.

Best Choice Early Answer

If you only buy the essential starter kit first, start with the legal-essentials core

The smartest first setup for most new notaries is not “everything on the page.” It is the minimum kit that lets you work confidently: journal, stamp, thumbprint pad, pens, a secure bag, and a phone workflow you can trust.

  • - Buy the legal essentials before the shiny extras
  • - Add organization and travel tools next
  • - Add scanner and dual-tray printer only when signing volume or goals justify them

Starter kit first, then scanner and printer only when loan signing work becomes real.

Loose certificates matter

Keep compliant loose certificates on hand, but buy them carefully. Wording can be state-specific, so this is one category where it usually makes more sense to follow your state rules than to copy a generic recommendation from someone else's supply list.

Backup supplies save appointments

Even if you do not need a specific recommendation here, keep backup stamp ink, extra pens, and basic refill-type supplies on hand. These are the small things that prevent a simple appointment from turning into a reschedule.

Business Tools

The items that help you book and serve more clients

Notary income does not come from gear alone. These tools help you market yourself, communicate clearly, and get paid efficiently.

Best for referrals

Business Cards

For newer notaries especially, business cards are still one of the easiest ways to generate word-of-mouth business.

  • - Simple, clean cards are usually easier to read and hand out than overdesigned ones.
  • - Useful for title offices, real estate contacts, and every signer you meet.

Operational essential

Cell Phone

This is the command center of a mobile notary business: calls, scheduling, directions, and follow-up all run through it.

  • - Keep key contacts saved and easy to reach.
  • - A dependable phone setup makes you easier to book and easier to trust.
Optional convenience

Tablet or Lightweight Admin Device

A tablet can be helpful for email, scheduling, invoicing, payments, and quick document review, but this is one category where personal workflow matters more than a hard recommendation.

  • - Helpful if you want a bigger screen than your phone without carrying a full laptop.
  • - The best choice here usually comes down to your preferred screen size, operating system, and how you like to work on the go.
