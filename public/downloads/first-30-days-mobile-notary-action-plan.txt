FIRST 30 DAYS AS A MOBILE NOTARY
Quick Action Plan from The Bearded Notary

WEEK 1: FOUNDATION AND SETUP
- Order your notary stamp, journal, business cards, thumbprint pad, and basic appointment supplies.
- Set up a business email, voicemail, and payment methods.
- Decide on your pricing for notarizations, travel, and after-hours work.
- Create your Google Business Profile and define your service area.
- Practice your notarization flow before taking live appointments.

WEEK 2: MARKETING AND TRAINING
- Create profiles on the top free business listing sites.
- Add your business to notary-specific directories.
- Start loan signing training if that fits your growth plan.
- Build your mobile notary kit and keep it ready to go.
- Make your first local outreach posts and direct contacts.

WEEK 3: FIRST CLIENTS AND REVIEWS
- Offer service to friends, family, and warm contacts.
- Complete your first notarizations and tighten your process.
- Ask every happy client for a Google review.
- Track which lead sources produce real appointments.
- Review your pricing and make small adjustments if needed.

WEEK 4: SCALE AND SYSTEMS
- Apply to signing companies if you are moving into loan signings.
- Expand local marketing that is already getting traction.
- Push toward your first 5 to 10 reviews.
- Measure income, signings, hours worked, and conversion sources.
- Set clear goals for month 2 and map your next actions.
