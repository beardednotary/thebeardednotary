# Content Backlog

Running bank of content ideas sourced from real recurring questions Ray sees in notary
Facebook groups / replies to the free download. Add to this list as new questions come up —
doesn't need to be scoped or prioritized at add-time, just captured so nothing gets lost.

Status legend: `idea` (not scoped) · `scoped` (ready to build) · `in progress` · `done`

---

## 1. Google Business Profile deep-dive
**Status:** idea
First 30 Days already has a "Days 5-6" section on this, but it's condensed. Real depth needed:
categories, photos, service-area setup, generating reviews without it looking fake, responding
to reviews, GBP posts — the stuff that actually moves "notary near me" ranking.

## 2. Going direct to title companies & law firms (client acquisition playbook)
**Status:** idea
First 30 Days has one cold-email template for law firms; target-industries-for-notaries lists
them as one of 15 industries but isn't tactical. Real gap: phone scripts, follow-up cadence,
objection handling, rosters (SigningAgent.com, SnapDocs, etc.), LinkedIn outreach — one real
playbook instead of scattered mentions. Also the natural home for "how do I get more business"
(both GNW and loan signings) rather than a separate page.

## 3. "Would you take this offer?" signing evaluator
**Status:** idea — strong candidate for a calculator, not an article
Recurring literal question in FB groups: given a flat fee + mileage + scan-backs + printing +
drive time, is this worth taking? Same pattern as the pricing and mileage calculators already
built this session — input the offer details, get an effective-hourly-rate verdict. Novel,
nothing like it exists on the site yet.

## 4. Is certification necessary? (LSS / NNA / Notary2Pro comparison)
**Status:** idea — needs care
why-now-is-perfect-time-loan-signing-agent argues LSS is worth it; nothing does a neutral
"do you actually need a cert, and which one" comparison. Site already earns LSS affiliate
income, so this needs the same honesty discipline as the income-claims rewrite — a real
comparison, not a funnel dressed as one.

## 5. E&O insurance — where to get it and how much
**Status:** idea
Cost range ($50-150/yr, $25K-$100K coverage) already stated in two places, but no page names
actual providers or answers "where do I actually go get this." Possible affiliate angle worth
checking once the page exists.

## 6. RON (remote online notarization) — how to get started
**Status:** idea
Zero mentions anywhere on the site currently. State RON endorsement/commission requirements
(different from a regular commission), platform sign-up (Notarize, Proof, OneNotary,
NotaryCam, Pavaso, etc.), webcam/ID-verification equipment needs.

## 7. Other services notaries can offer
**Status:** idea
Apostille, I-9 verification (mentioned once in passing), fingerprinting, wedding officiant
work. Diversify-your-income angle.

## 8. Balancing mobile notary work with parenting / a full-time job
**Status:** idea
Softer, more community/trust content than SEO-driven, but real relatability — reinforces the
"written by someone who actually did this" positioning.

## 9. How to fill out your notary journal
**Status:** idea
The journal is recommended constantly across the site, but nothing walks through an actual
filled-in entry. Concrete, practical, zero current depth.

## 10. How to generate invoices
**Status:** idea
Invoicing is *mentioned* everywhere (which software supports it, when to use one vs. collect
on the spot) but nothing shows how to actually build one — no template, no walkthrough of what
fields a notary invoice needs (service date, fee breakdown, late fee terms, EIN if applicable).
Pairs with the QuickBooks affiliate link already in place, and Wave/FreshBooks if those ever
get affiliate links too.

## 11. "Should I have refused?" / can I refuse a job
**Status:** idea
Recurring question about declining offers or backing out mid-appointment — red flags (signer
seems confused/coerced, bad ID, sketchy document requests, safety concerns), and what a notary
is actually allowed to refuse. Fits the site's real-experience positioning well.
